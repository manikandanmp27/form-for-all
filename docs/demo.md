# Hackathon Demonstration Guide

This guide provides a concise 2–3 minute demonstration flow for showcasing **Fill-For-Me (FormForAll)** to judges, evaluators, or users.

---

## Demo Flow

### 1. Open Application
* **Presenter Action**: Open the web application at the homepage (`/`).
* **Audience Takeaway**: Notice the clean, modern interface and accessible top navigation bar.

### 2. Select/Setup Accessibility Preference
* **Presenter Action**: Open the top bar Accessibility Profile selector and enable **Low Vision / High Contrast Mode** or **Dyslexia Friendly Font**.
* **Audience Takeaway**: Observe how contrast ratios, font typography, and focus indicators adapt instantly across all pages.

### 3. Upload Sample Form
* **Presenter Action**: Navigate to the Dashboard (`/dashboard`), click **Upload Form File**, select a sample PDF or image form, and click **Create Accessible Session**.
* **Audience Takeaway**: Physical paper forms or non-interactive PDFs are ingested into the platform in seconds.

### 4. Show Field Extraction
* **Presenter Action**: Show the extracted interactive form fields generated by backend Gemini Vision AI.
* **Audience Takeaway**: Complex document layouts are automatically converted into structured, screen-reader friendly digital input fields.

### 5. Start Guided Filling
* **Presenter Action**: Click into an active field to begin sequential form completion.
* **Audience Takeaway**: Step-by-step interactive guidance replaces dense, overwhelming form pages.

### 6. Demonstrate Plain-Language Explanation & Voice Assistant
* **Presenter Action**: Click **Listen Field** to hear help guidance spoken aloud, and click **Voice Input (Microphone)** to speak an answer.
* **Audience Takeaway**: Visually impaired or low-literacy users can complete forms hands-free through speech.

### 7. Trigger a Risk-Aware Warning
* **Presenter Action**: Open the **Privacy & Risk Audit Drawer** on a field requesting sensitive data (e.g. password or bank PIN).
* **Audience Takeaway**: Real-time security analysis flags excessive or dangerous personal data requests to protect users from fraud.

### 8. Confirm & Review Answers
* **Presenter Action**: Review completed responses in the summary view.
* **Audience Takeaway**: Clear review step ensures answer accuracy before final submission.

### 9. Submit/Download
* **Presenter Action**: Click **Export Completed Form** to generate the final PDF document.
* **Audience Takeaway**: Seamless end-to-end transformation from raw document to final completed submission.
